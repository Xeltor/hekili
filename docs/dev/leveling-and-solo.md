# Leveling and PvE development

This fork extends Hekili Forever's existing endgame rotations across levels 1–60. Upstream's max-level damage priorities and WoWSims replays are the baseline for sustained group and raid fights.

## Rotation changes

- Use Icy Veins' WoW Forever guides for leveling priorities where available.
- Build below-cap fallbacks around learned spells and ranks, available talents, and valid weapons, forms, stances, or pets. Use the existing `leveling` and `levelingFirst` recipe hooks when they fit. Confirm that the fallbacks stay inactive in the max-level baseline.
- Set `supportsLeveling = true` on an individual spec with a leveling rotation, or on the class kit when all its builds support leveling. The below-60 warning follows the active spec.
- Add interrupts, recovery, and defensives where their conditions justify them in PvE. Use outdoor context for choices such as short-pull rage spending and armor stacks. Apply those rules in regular parties as well as solo play. Use client evidence or the combat harness to verify the conditions.
- Extend the shipped rotation in its priority source or recipe, and regenerate the Lua pack using the existing tooling. Use shared code for behavior that belongs to multiple classes and keep class-specific decisions in the class pack.
- Cover a representative early kit, the unlock that changes the decision, and solo and group cases when relevant. Compare max-level group and raid recommendations against upstream with the same inputs. Use the existing headless scenarios and replay gates. Record the level, spec, talents, and encounter context for in-game checks, and keep upstream replay thresholds intact.

See the [upstream development guide](../../DEVELOPMENT.md), [pack authoring guide](wowsims-packs.md), and [in-game ledger](ingame-ledger.md) for the existing tools and checks.

### PvE interrupt priorities

Druid, Warrior, Rogue, and Protection Paladin interrupt recommendations share a catalog drawn from Forever logs and encounter references. A readable priority spell can get an interrupt cue even when the caster is new to the catalog. A known caster in range can also make Hekili save the interrupt, provided the caster is fighting you or your group. That check includes visible enemies whose identity is readable. An unlisted mob still gets the normal interrupt cue when there is no reachable priority caster. If the selected known caster's spell is hidden, its normal cue remains available unless another reachable enemy has a confirmed priority cast. ## Feral Druid leveling and tanking

The Feral and Guardian packs share their Cat and Bear lists. Before completing the Bear Form quest, pull with Wrath and use learned Moonfire while moving or fighting. Bear covers the levels before Cat Form and Claw. Outdoors, a single enemy favors Cat. A second distinct, engaged melee enemy can warrant Bear in solo play or an unassigned party. A low-health solo Cat under direct attack can also get a Bear recommendation. Keep Bear through the rest of that fight and reassess on the next pull. Single-target mode suppresses pack-based shifts. [Icy Veins Feral leveling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/feral-druid-leveling).

**Druid form cues.** Selecting a living hostile target prepares Cat or Bear according to your role and learned abilities. Balance prepares a learned Moonkin Form. Out of combat in the open world, ground movement can cue Travel Form outdoors or learned Cat Form indoors and before Travel is learned. While moving toward a target confirmed beyond ten yards, the movement form takes priority. At ten yards or closer it switches to the combat form. Swimming without a hostile target can cue Aquatic. Travel forms are manual in dungeons and raids, where selecting an enemy can cue the combat form immediately. Entering combat ends movement cues, and a group tank can get a Bear cue even before selecting an enemy. These cues can appear on Primary when display visibility would hide the queue.

**Group tanking.** Use `/hekili spec guardian` for dungeons and questing. Select the Tank role in groups. Enable **Interrupts** and **Defensives**. Enable **Allow Target Swaps** under **Targeting** and turn on enemy nameplates to see cues for another mob. A tank in Bear with 20 Rage or less can prepare Enrage before pulling a selected idle dungeon enemy within 30 yards and beyond melee. Before learning Bear, a tank can open with a trained caster spell. Faerie Fire becomes the ranged Bear opener once trained at 18. Once anyone in the group is fighting, single-target attacks require evidence that the mob is engaged with the group. A wounded tank stays in Bear even when the healer enters combat first. In both packs, a damage-assigned Druid can prepare Cat between pulls and an assigned healer keeps control of form choice.

**Bear threat and damage.** Growl can pick up a mob chasing a living non-tank before it hits the Druid. Bash can interrupt that caster in melee and talented Feral Charge can interrupt from 8–25 yards. Challenging Roar can recover two or more confirmed loose dungeon enemies attacking non-tanks within ten yards. Boss handoffs stay with the tanks. A held enemy can justify Demoralizing Roar. Early Bear spends Rage on Maul against one mob. From level 16, Swipe takes priority when two or more living hostile NPCs are confirmed in melee range. With an idle mob selected, another nearby mob must be confirmed fighting the party. With Lacerate trained, Bear maintains five stacks on one target. Once the tank holds the pack, it can maintain Lacerate on the selected elite or boss and use available Primal Bites. With target swaps enabled, it can also spread its own Lacerate to another held elite in melee or Faerie Fire to an engaged enemy. Maul uses excess Rage above 35 with the advanced single-target kit and above 50 on a pack with Swipe or the advanced kit. AoE uses confirmed range and leaves crowd-control decisions to the tank. Mobs at unknown range do not affect the recommendation. If the selected mob dies or is idle, select the surviving attacker for a single-target pickup. [Icy Veins Feral rotation](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/feral-druid-melee-dps-and-tank-pve-rotation-cooldowns-abilities) and [Wowhead tank rotation](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/druid/feral/rotation-cooldowns-pve-tank).

**Cat damage.** On ordinary pulls, Cat uses a trained builder it can afford. Shred uses evidence that another unit has the enemy's attention or the player's behind-target setting. Claw works from the front. When the selected enemy has a measured lifetime of at least six seconds, an outdoor Cat can apply Rip at three points. A Cat dealing damage in a leveling dungeon waits for five. With three seconds or less left, four points can go into Ferocious Bite. Without a usable estimate, a durable enemy can warrant Rip at four points, ordinary enemies at five, and visible low health favors direct damage. With another party member tanking, Cat can suggest spreading Rake after bleeding the priority target and spending its combo points. An alternate needs a readable missing bleed and confirmed range. The level-60 raid rotation retains its own energy pooling and finisher timings. [Wowhead hybrid tank leveling guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/druid/feral/tank-leveling-tips) and [Wowhead Cat leveling guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/druid/feral/dps-leveling-tips).

**Recovery.** Defensives covers in-form cooldowns, carried healing consumables and between-pull self-healing. Barkskin uses a 60% health threshold. Frenzied Regeneration uses 25% with at least 10 Rage, or 55% with 30 Rage when holding multiple dungeon enemies after repeated heavy hits. These thresholds are fork tuning. When combat health is hidden, the client displays eligible health cues. Between pulls, a Druid at or below 75% health can recover with Healing Touch or Regrowth. With a living enemy selected, an idle pull allows Rejuvenation in caster form at that threshold and reserves Healing Touch for 40% health or lower. Once the enemy starts fighting the party, the preparation rules stop even before the Druid enters combat. A solo caster who has yet to learn Bear can still use Rejuvenation at 55% health or lower and Healing Touch at 40% or lower in combat. Feral and Guardian self-care follows the configured display visibility, including Target and Combat with Target. In caster form it can maintain Mark and Thorns, clear an observed poison or curse and prepare Regrowth before an outdoor elite when enough mana remains to shift. Targeted group utility such as Rebirth and Innervate still needs a player-selected recipient. [Barkskin](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/22812) and [Frenzied Regeneration](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/22842).

At zero Rage, start swinging if auto-attack is off. With swings running, the next Rage spender may show a wait until the swing pays for it. A positive check for the actual spell can establish melee range even when the distance library reports zero to eight yards. A hidden target GUID can still support an attack or Growl when the selected unit provides direct evidence of reach, combat and threat.

For client checks, use the same Guardian profile for an outdoor single pull, two engaged melee enemies, and a dungeon pull with one enemy attacking the healer. Record the assigned roles, trained ranks, visible nameplates, target-swap setting, and whether Defensives is separate. The level-60 replays use the upstream sustained fights.

## Warrior priorities and equipment

Stance changes can discard banked Rage. Learned Tactical Mastery retains up to 10 Rage from level 14. Each rank of Improved Tactical Mastery raises that limit by 5, up to 25 with both the passive and all three ranks. The cost of the follow-up attack matters as much as the cost of switching.

**Arms** usually stays in Battle Stance while leveling. Use Rend on ordinary enemies, time an untalented Slam just after a main-hand swing, and give learned Spearing Strike priority against Giants and Dragonkin. Outside raids and level-60 bosses, a Whirlwind stance change needs four confirmed nearby enemies, a ready Whirlwind, a melee weapon, and enough retained Rage to cast it. The fork budgets at most 15 Rage lost on the switch, the base cost of a Slam. Battle Stance becomes useful again once the Whirlwind opportunity passes. The level-40 leveling scenario uses the guide build. The raid replay uses the pinned simulator build. [Arms leveling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/arms-warrior-leveling/) and [Whirlwind spell data](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/1680).

**Fury** uses Rend and low-Rage Overpower early. Thunder Clap helps against nearby packs. Once Whirlwind and Berserker Stance are available, the nonraid damage priority favors staying in Berserker for Whirlwind and Bloodthirst. An available Bloodthirst or short-pull Execute can spend banked Rage before the switch. After a dodge against a single enemy, a brief return to Battle can pay off when Overpower fits the window, the switch preserves Rage, and neither main attack needs the next global cooldown. A missing stance quest keeps the early rotation available. Before Whirlwind, a normal enemy still engaged after 12 observed seconds can merit up to two Sunders while outside Execute range. Durable enemies are candidates for Sunder after the main attacks have their Rage. Health-spending Rage generators take existing Gore Drinker healing into account. [Fury leveling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/fury-warrior-leveling/) and [Fury endgame guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/fury-warrior-melee-dps-pve-rotation-cooldowns-abilities).

**Protection** gives an available Victory Rush priority on a controlled pull when its full heal will fit or the kill window is about to close. Ordinary dungeon enemies get less routine Rend and single-target Thunder Clap upkeep. If a shieldless Warrior is actually tanking a dungeon enemy, Defensive Stance can still provide mitigation and access to Revenge. The cue requires the mob to be attacking the Warrior, confirmed tank threat and a switch that preserves banked Rage. Hostile training dummies use the sustained rotation. [Protection leveling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/protection-warrior-leveling/) and [Protection endgame guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/protection-warrior-tank-pve-rotation-cooldowns-abilities).

**Choosing a shield mid-fight.** Keep a damage weapon equipped for normal Arms or Fury damage, including stance changes for Whirlwind. Equip a one-hander and shield before a planned tank pickup or dangerous damage phase. During a fight, the switch can pay for itself when picking up a dangerous add, covering a tank death, or preparing Shield Wall against a known incoming hit. For an important interrupt, check the current loadout first. Pummel works in Berserker Stance from level 38. Shield Bash works in Battle or Defensive Stance with a shield from level 12. An emergency swap for Bash needs enough time left for the equip and the interrupt. Blizzard allows both weapon slots to change in one global cooldown. The pinned simulator also resets swing timers on a combat weapon change. Hekili follows equipment changes made by the player. Its model of an enemy cast end and its view of currently equipped gear cannot establish whether a particular swap will land in time. [Blizzard's weapon-swap notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-november-2/2360696/5), [WoWSims weapon-swap implementation](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/ab0f00e4fc83/sim/core/item_swaps.go#L370-L374) and [Fury utility guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/fury-warrior-melee-dps-pve-rotation-cooldowns-abilities).

**PvP** interrupts use the current hostile player's observed interruptible cast, confirmed melee range, stance, equipment and the Interrupts toggle. Arms favors Mortal Strike pressure and can slow a visibly moving opponent. Fury leads with an eligible Execute or Bloodthirst. Protection can use a Revenge proc, Shield Slam, Victory Rush and an eligible Execute without assuming NPC threat rules. The player chooses interrupt targets, tank assignments and equipment. [Arms PvP guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warrior-pvp-guide).

All three packs can suggest Attack when swings are explicitly off and an eligible target is in melee range. Arms and Fury can use Charge on an outdoor pull or to join an engaged dungeon target when the learned rank confirms range. Incoming casts and emergency abilities take precedence during combat.

Protection's leveling priority follows the [Icy Veins Protection guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/protection-warrior-leveling). It checks stance, shield, the Revenge proc, Rage and encounter context before picking abilities. Its health-triggered recommendations use the same Classic Primary display as Arms.

Protection also recommends the learned Attack action (6603) when the client explicitly reports autoattack off. It costs no Rage or global cooldown, so an eligible melee target gets the reminder before Bloodrage and routine Rage-spending buffs, after ready interrupts, major defensives and stance preparation. A selected outdoor target can receive a pull cue; dungeon targets must already be engaged with the player or party. Raids retain their existing priority. A simulation-only aura prevents repeated Attack icons in one forecast, and each new prediction checks the real flag again. The addon recommends the player's Attack input; it never calls a protected attack command. Focused checks cover zero Rage, initial Rage spent on buffs, the GCD, queue deduplication, explicit-off versus unknown flags, range, weapons, target eligibility, and level-1 outdoor pulls.

Charge, its stance preparation and damaging abilities share that target-eligibility rule. An idle dungeon target stays ineligible while the player fights something else; a positively engaged party target can receive a joining cue. Invalid or unreadable targets cannot select the sustained raid branch merely because the nonraid predicate is false. Learned normal and Vanguard Charge ranks are checked at their rank boundaries.

Between casts, Shield Bash reserves Rage only for NPC types positively observed with an interruptible cast or channel. A cast start alone is insufficient evidence. Current-cast handling remains separate, and a known mixed caster retains its reserve after an uninterruptible spell. Shield Block also considers a confirmed nearby melee enemy attacking the player while the selected target is casting. A temporary Concussion Blow immunity expires after 30 seconds; this conservative retry policy does not claim to measure the server's diminishing-return reset.

All Warrior stance changes cap the lower, estimated and upper Rage values independently at the learned retention limit. They neither add Rage below that limit nor treat the cap as an ordinary spend. Actual observations and forecasts use the same operation.

At 80 or more confirmed Rage, Protection queues Heroic Strike (one enemy) or Cleave (multiple enemies) before filler Rend and Sunder Armor. This threshold leaves room for incoming Rage while preserving the guide's main abilities, interrupt and mitigation reserves, and ordinary end-of-pull pooling. Level-28 regression cases cover solo and grouped pulls, lower Rage, Revenge, Shield Block, interrupts, and range. The sustained raid priority is unchanged.

In nonraid PvE, Thunder Clap repeats when ready against nearby packs. Thunder Clap, Cleave, Demoralizing Shout and Sweeping Strikes use confirmed range for nearby enemies and leave crowd-control decisions to the tank. Its interrupt reserve remains in force. Normal and rare dungeon enemies use the same one-stack routine Sunder budget as ordinary outdoor enemies; elites and bosses retain five-stack upkeep. Immediate threat recovery can still spend additional Sunders. Classification is a conservative policy for pull duration, not a measurement of hidden target health.

**Allow Target Swaps** lets Protection suggest missing Rend on durable enemies, a first Sunder on grouped enemies, or a switch from a securely held enemy to a loose melee enemy attacking a groupmate. Thunder Clap takes priority when it can reach the mob. After a switch, Shield Slam, Revenge and Sunder build threat even on an enemy with five Sunders. Once the mob is under control the regular damage and mitigation priorities resume. Solo play has no group-threat recovery. The same recovery handles outdoor parties, dungeon packs and identifiable raid trash between encounters. Raid tanks and boss handoffs remain player assignments. [Icy Veins' Forever Protection rotation](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/protection-warrior-tank-pve-rotation-cooldowns-abilities).

In a party with unassigned roles, holding one enemy securely gives Protection evidence that the Warrior is tanking. Raid groups require a tank assignment and an identifiable healer or damage dealer on the loose enemy. Enabled emergency defensives and interrupts take priority. Under incoming melee pressure, Shield Block can precede recovery if there is enough Rage left to act on the loose enemy. A controlled short fight can use Execute from Battle or Berserker Stance when the Warrior can spare the Rage.

Taunt matches the highest threat and forces the enemy to attack the Warrior briefly. Keep attacking to hold the lead. The fork treats Taunt as a last resort in Defensive Stance. Two observed casts of Shield Slam, Revenge or Sunder on the same loose enemy must leave it attacking the same groupmate 1.5 seconds after the second cast, all within eight seconds of the first. Switching targets or recovering threat clears the evidence. A forecast cast or a lack of Rage cannot start the timer. [Wowhead's Forever Protection abilities](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warrior/protection/tank-abilities).

Reckless Swings follows the guide's health-to-spare advice with a conservative fork policy: readable health of at least 75%, proven Rage at most 25, active melee autoattacks, and an elite or boss outside the Execute window. It requires the actual learned talent rather than a fixed character level. Hidden health blocks this recommendation. The old immediate 20-Rage grant was removed from the shared Warrior model; proper per-swing charge accounting remains open (CN15), so bonus Rage may be underestimated until a client observation reconciles it. This also affects Fury builds using the talent.

Focused checks exercise a legal learned-spell recommendation at every level 1–60, plus selected ability/rank boundaries, solo/group/dungeon/raid contexts, allowed and blocked utility, optional target swaps, cooldown startup, and resource accounting. The November 14 review passed 475 focused checks, Protection's pinned replay, level-10/40/60 live harness scenarios, and the full 20-fight Protection loop with no runtime errors or refused actions. The pinned replay retains its 0.92 on-GCD agreement floor. This is not proof of every talent build or encounter. Incoming damage-based Rage hidden by the client remains open (CN7/CN13). Disarm remains manual. In-game checks remain separate from harness validation.

The November 14 review found two broader Warrior checks already failing with the previous stance-spend implementation: the Arms level-60 live scenario idles for 6.2 seconds against its 6.0-second limit, and Fury's pinned timing snapshot differs at the 3.77-second Heroic Strike landing. Both reproduce with only the new stance-cap callers restored to their old behavior. Their thresholds and snapshots remain unchanged; the complete Warrior suite is not green.

## Protection Paladin

An ordinary solo pull uses Seal of Righteousness, a melee weapon and the trained rank of Holy Strike. On a durable enemy or an engaged pack, a shield makes tank abilities worthwhile. Holy Shield helps when the Paladin is taking melee pressure. Optional damage spells leave mana for the next Holy Strike and, under shield pressure with **Defensives** enabled, Holy Shield. The pack preserves an aura the player already chose. [Wowhead Protection leveling guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/protection/tank-leveling-tips)

Protection maintains Righteous Fury once trained (level 10 in Forever), including solo and before a pull with no enemy selected. Assigned tanks keep it while another tank holds the boss. A Paladin assigned to heal or deal damage skips automatic threat-buff upkeep. [Blizzard’s Forever tuning notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-november-2/2360696/1)

Group tanks use Seal of Fury once learned. Consecration can pick up a nearby pack when there is mana to sustain it and enemies are confirmed in reach. The tank decides whether to break nearby crowd control. **Allow Target Swaps** lets the Paladin recover an engaged mob attacking a groupmate in outdoor groups, dungeons and between raid bosses. The rotation tries direct Holy damage on a loose mob. If two observed attacks on that mob leave it attacking the same groupmate, the Paladin can judge Seal of Fury to taunt. A living groupmate at 25% health or lower can trigger an immediate rescue when their health is readable. If the seal needs changing, the rotation checks the mana for both the seal and Judgement. [Blizzard’s Paladin class changes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302099)

Raid bosses use the sustained WoWSims priority. A Paladin holding the boss can judge Fury for damage. While another tank holds it, the pack suppresses that taunting Judgement. Boss handoffs stay with the players. [Icy Veins Protection rotation](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/protection-paladin-tank-pve-rotation-cooldowns-abilities)

**Self-healing.** With **Defensives** enabled, a stationary Paladin between pulls can use Holy Light at 50% health or below. Up to 80%, Flash of Light is the cheaper, quicker choice when trained and affordable, with Holy Light available from level 1. Drinking or eating holds these suggestions. During combat, a lone Paladin can heal against a single enemy and can use Hammer of Justice to buy the cast time. At 25% or below against several confirmed solo enemies, a trained heal is a last resort if instant defenses are unavailable. A group tank takes a casting window when the opponent is stunned or the Paladin already has immunity long enough to finish, provided no loose mob needs attention. An off-tank can heal when another tank has the enemy. At 15% health or below, Lay on Hands is an instant emergency heal. It spends the caster’s remaining mana, then restores 250 mana to the recipient at rank 2 or 550 at rank 3. The first rank has no mana return. [Wowhead Protection leveling](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/protection/tank-leveling-tips) · [Icy Veins Paladin healing spells](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/holy-paladin-leveling/) · [WoWSims Lay on Hands](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/ab0f00e4fc83/sim/paladin/lay_on_hands.go)

**Damage and threat.** The fork offers Templar’s Bulwark at 45% health in combat, or at 65% when three visible melee enemies are attacking the Paladin or a recently observed hit took at least a quarter of maximum health. It can still help if the selected target disappears. Divine Shield and its early alternative, Divine Protection, are emergency options at 20% only in a confirmed solo outdoor fight. Enemies can attack a groupmate during immunity. The raid tank controls planned bubble-and-taunt or debuff-clearing plays. Blessing of Freedom can help a rooted or snared Paladin get back to an engaged enemy, and Cleanse or Purify can treat an observed removable effect on a solo outdoor pull. These health thresholds are fork tuning. [Wowhead tank cooldowns](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/protection/rotation-cooldowns-pve-tank) · [Blizzard Paladin class changes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302099/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-paladin)

**Displaying and targeting a heal.** A combat-only Primary display can show an eligible self-heal between pulls while its offensive queue stays hidden. Separating **Defensives** sends the cue to that display. Self-heals require a binding or native targeting setup that sends the spell to the Paladin. WoW’s **Auto Self Cast** normally handles an enemy or empty target. An explicitly bound `/cast [@player] Holy Light` macro can also keep the enemy selected.

## Rogue leveling and PvE

Assassination, Combat, and Subtlety extend their default packs using the Icy Veins leveling guides. Ordinary solo and party pulls give direct finishers more weight, while dungeon elites favor sustained buffs and bleeds. Combat uses Riposte and cleaves with Blade Flurry when a second melee target is available. Assassination falls back from Mutilate when either dagger is missing. Subtlety builds with Hemorrhage when learned and uses Rupture, Death Mark, and the Ghostly Strike–Garrote setup on durable targets. Raids and level-60 bosses use the pinned WoWSims damage priority. [Assassination guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/assassination-rogue-leveling/), [Combat guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/combat-rogue-leveling/), [Subtlety guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/subtlety-rogue-leveling/).

Outdoor pulls can suggest Stealth when there is an eligible opener, or Attack when autoattacking is explicitly off. Already being in Stealth with an equipped weapon and a selected living hostile NPC in melee range expresses intent to attack, including on an idle dungeon target. The queue offers a learned opener and falls back to an eligible strike or finisher instead of waiting indefinitely. Unknown positioning does not block a stealthed opener; a visibly engaged enemy targeting the player blocks the behind-target assumption unless **Always Behind** is enabled. Weapon requirements, immunity, stun spacing, and range still apply. Poison preparation and reserving Energy for Kick do not displace this opener. A forecast may continue after its simulated opening attack, but that simulated engagement is discarded before the next live update. Unstealthed dungeon pulls still wait for engagement. The sustained in-combat raid priority retains its own Vanish windows. Target classification guides finisher choice because target lifetime is unavailable.

Kick and the fallback Kidney Shot honor the Interrupts toggle. The stun cue requires a normal or rare target and respects tracked immunity. **Stun Spacing** defaults to a 30-second minimum between your own attempts on the same enemy; zero disables that spacing. This is a conservative recommendation policy, not a measured diminishing-return reset timer. History follows actual casts, survives combat end, and uses bounded suppression when target identity is hidden. A matched temporary stun immunity expires rather than marking the enemy immune for its entire life. Other players' stuns remain unknown. [Blizzard's November 13 hotfixes](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-13-2026) confirm PvE stun diminishing returns but do not specify a reset interval.

Evasion, Feint, healing potions, and Healthstones honor Defensives. Solo Evasion remains proactive; it also considers confirmed nearby attackers when the selected enemy is casting or controlled. Group Feint includes recovery after taking full aggro. Blade Flurry requires positive melee range and engagement with the player or party for an additional enemy. Unknown range or identity does not prove another cleave target. This is not a guarantee that every nearby crowd-control effect is visible.

**Poison Reminders** is independent of the Potions toggle. Each hand offers Automatic, Instant Poison, Deadly Poison, or No Reminder. Automatic uses Instant on the main hand and prefers Deadly on the off hand against durable targets, falling back to Instant when needed. Reminders choose a usable rank in the bags and preserve existing coatings.

**Continuous Combat** is off by default. Enable it to maintain Slice and Dice against ordinary enemies when you expect to keep attacking across target changes. The default short-pull policy and pinned raid priorities remain in place. This setting supplies player intent rather than claiming to measure hidden enemy lifespan. Subtlety can spend Ghostly Strike's allowance on an eligible Ambush when Garrote is unsuitable; Initiative's guaranteed bonus is included in the forecast. Without Boot Knife, Shank may recover Energy at five combo points only when it is affordable and its recovery beats waiting for a finisher.

Racial recommendations require the player's race, learned spell, cooldown and applicable toggle. Nonraid damage boosts use Blood Fury, Berserking, Elune's Light or Eureka on durable or multi-enemy pulls, or with Continuous Combat enabled, with enough confirmed Energy to use their window. Raid damage priorities remain pinned upstream. Will to Survive, Will of the Forsaken and Escape Artist respond only to readable matching control effects; loss-of-control rows are cached on events rather than queried during each prediction. Stoneform and Shatter Curse react to readable removable effects, and Stoneform can also mitigate nearby melee pressure at low health. Rapid Regeneration and Read Ley Line supply stationary, out-of-combat recovery cues. The latter's health-regeneration benefit applies to Rogues even though they have no mana. These utility recommendations honor Defensives; Read Ley Line also retains its shared Cooldowns toggle. See the [racial evidence and limits](../research/2026-11-14-rogue-racials.md) and [Blizzard's racial changes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-november-2/2360696/7).

Perception, Shadowmeld, Skysight, Walk on Air and Cannibalize remain manual: undiscovered enemies, encounter resets, movement intent, fall safety and a usable nearby corpse cannot all be established by the supported observations. Skysight is registered for supported custom priorities, including its cast-time movement restriction. Tauren is not a supported Rogue race, so War Stomp is not a Rogue fallback.

Focused scenarios cover learned talent builds, whole opener/builder/finisher transitions, Ghostly Strike follow-ups, weapon and target changes, interrupt recovery, hidden values, racial ownership and utility, and the new settings. The every-level builder sweep proves fallback availability only. Full replay/loop validation and live position, poison, crowd-control and display checks remain separate requirements. Manual threat drops and raid Expose Armor assignments still need player judgment.

## Warlock leveling, PvE and PvP

Affliction, SM Ruin, Demonology, Shadow Brand and Destruction use the character's learned ranks from levels 1–60. On healthy ordinary enemies before level 10, Immolate and Corruption can both pay off. From level 10 the shadow builds generally open short pulls with Corruption. The rotation needs a readable health measurement to choose a finisher by enemy health. When Forever hides that measurement, the Classic display can still let the client show a low-health Drain Soul cue. All five builds check movement using each spell's current cast time, including in boss fights and custom priorities. Five ranks of Improved Corruption allow Corruption on the move, and Nightfall allows an instant Shadow Bolt. Wrack, drains, Rain of Fire and other channels need a stationary caster. A shorter Soul Fire or summon still needs the player to stop. While moving on a boss, a healthy Warlock can use Life Tap when low on mana or finish with a spare-shard Shadowburn when eligible. A root still permits stationary casting. An observed silence suppresses ordinary spells, and a stun or fear also suppresses Shoot. [Method leveling guide](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/wow-forever-warlock-leveling-guide-and-talents) and [Blizzard's class deep dive](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24310968/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-rogue-and-warlock).

**Affliction questing.** From levels 10–59 on non-trivial outdoor mobs, Hekili tracks your Corruption and Bane of Agony independently. Corruption has priority when both spells are missing. Agony can follow whenever it is missing, and an active Agony gets its final, strongest tick before Hekili calls for another. At level 11, wand while both spells tick. When you need a shard, switch to Drain Soul to finish the mob and let the channel run even if a DoT expires. If you can see the mob will die in a few wand shots, skip the refresh yourself. [Wowhead's Affliction leveling rotation](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/affliction/dps-leveling-tips) and [Icy Veins' single-target leveling rotation](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/affliction-warlock-leveling).

**Wands and drains.** On ordinary outdoor pulls, let the DoTs tick. Early on, the choice between a learned rank 1 or 2 Drain Life and a wand uses the equipped wand's damage and speed, Shadow spell power, and relevant drain talents. A tie favors the mana-free wand. Rank 3 Drain Life, trainable at 30, becomes a useful sustain filler. Affliction uses it before learning Wrack and goes back to it from Wrack when healing or mana matters. Fire Demonology reapplies Demonic Brand on its demon's target and can wand or drain between applications. The direct-cast builds keep their learned filler when healthy and able to afford it. An ordinary enemy already near death favors a finisher or wand. [Affliction](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/affliction/dps-leveling-tips), [Demonology](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/demonology/dps-leveling-tips), and [Destruction leveling guides](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/destruction/dps-leveling-tips).

**Taking hits.** A matching delayed player cast can favor sustain for the next five seconds while the same outdoor enemy is in confirmed melee range and targeting the Warlock. When the next filler would be Shadow Bolt, the packs compare a wand's observed damage per second with the trained Bolt's estimated damage over its cast time plus the delay measured on the last Bolt. Nightfall's instant Bolt takes priority. The comparison needs readable timing and a recorded gear snapshot. Future hits and the target's resistances are unknown. Fel Concentration helps Drain Life retain its ticks under fire. Intensity protects Destruction casts. Both talents reach 70% protection at full rank. [Fel Concentration](https://mattjcr.github.io/wowforeverultimatecalculator/en/classes/warlock/talents/fel-concentration/) and [Intensity](https://mattjcr.github.io/wowforeverultimatecalculator/en/classes/warlock/talents/intensity/).

**Mana and recovery.** Life Tap is trainable at level 6. During field combat, after affordable DoTs, Hekili can suggest it before wanding when mana is below 50% and the learned Tap would leave at least 55% player health. The pack uses these thresholds to recover mana before the next DoT. The guides recommend tapping while DoTs tick or while moving, when health is available. Classic can show the Tap when combat health is hidden. WoW applies the health threshold using the observed maximum health and the last confirmed Spirit for the current gear. A change to stats invalidates the cost until a fresh observation. [Icy Veins on Life Tap](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/affliction-warlock-leveling).

With Defensives enabled, an engaged outdoor target in Drain Life range can justify one Life Tap before a rank 3+ drain when mana is below 30%, health is above 85% and the Tap leaves a safe reserve. Drain Life needs a stationary caster and mana for the trained rank. An active wand can give way to a useful drain. Shoot requires a learned, usable action, an equipped wand and confirmed range. The display tracks automatic shooting between shots. Stopping the wand during a global cooldown keeps the next due spell first. The pack also holds a wand restart or filler cast if it would delay a queued debuff refresh the Warlock can already afford. An equipped melee weapon is a last resort against a PvE enemy already in confirmed melee range. With a separate Defensives display, routine draining belongs on the damage display and emergency healing cues go to Defensives. [Rank 3 Drain Life](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/709) and [Method's Warlock leveling guide](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/wow-forever-warlock-leveling-guide-and-talents).

**Dungeons and sustained damage.** In the Affliction pack, apply Corruption to each engaged dungeon mob in spell range. With **Allow Target Swaps** enabled under **Targeting**, the full-size target-swap icon appears when another eligible enemy still needs Corruption. Select an undotted mob and follow the updated recommendation. The cue uses visible enemies, tracked applications and observed crowd control. With swaps off, you can tab manually. Before Wrack, a usable wand fills the gaps on dungeon trash. A regular Shadow Bolt must leave mana for another cast of your trained Corruption. Optional spells such as Agony and Immolate also leave that mana when another eligible mob still needs Corruption. [Wowhead's dungeon rotation](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/affliction/dps-leveling-tips).

Dungeon damage waits for evidence of engagement with the player, their demon or their party, and pauses on a selected mob with observed Fear, Howl of Terror or Banish. Durable enemies get the assigned Curse and the build's DoTs. Amplify Curse can precede an in-range Bane of Agony, which runs to expiration. Destruction can put Bane of Havoc on a second enemy. Rain of Fire requires three distinct engaged enemies in reach and a stationary caster. The player checks the ground placement and nearby crowd control. Raid damage and sustained level-60 boss damage follow the pinned WoWSims lists.

**Spec differences.** Affliction can fill with Wrack after applying its DoTs. SM Ruin uses Shadow Bolt for direct damage. Both Demonology variants can use Searing Pain for Demonic Brand while their demon attacks the selected enemy. Fire Brand uses its Decimation proc for Soul Fire, and Shadow Brand uses Shadow Bolt for Revelation. Destruction times Conflagrate around Immolate's expiration, a visible finishing opportunity or full Shadow and Flame. A learned Shadowburn can finish a field enemy with a spare shard. At full Shadow and Flame, Destruction can use the last shard without assuming a refund in its prediction. [Affliction](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/affliction/rotation-cooldowns-pve-dps), [Demonology](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/demonology/rotation-cooldowns-pve-dps) and [Destruction](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/destruction/dps-leveling-tips) guides.

**Defensives.** All five builds offer a usable Healthstone at 30% health or below and a healing potion at 40% or below. If neither is available, a learned Death Coil can provide an instant heal at 40% or below. At up to 65%, after checking for a needed Shadow Ward or Soul Link, a stationary Warlock fighting an NPC prefers Drain Life and can use Death Coil if Drain Life cannot reach or is unavailable. When moving or fighting a player, Death Coil takes priority at that threshold. The spells require a confirmed engaged target in range and respect observed breakable control. Turning off **Defensives** holds both spells. With Defensives on the main display, four of the upstream boss lists can also use Death Coil as an end-of-fight damage spell. The separate Defensives display reserves it for healing. Classic can show an eligible health cue on the selected display using the client's protected health rendering. Drain Life heals the Warlock. If the wand's global cooldown is the only delay, its icon stays visible with a cooldown swipe. [Death Coil](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/6789).

A learned Shadow Ward can absorb a follow-up hit when the client reports pure Shadow damage to the Warlock in the last five seconds. Health Funnel, Life Tap and mana rune self-damage are excluded around their observed use. Soul Link can be restored during combat, even while moving, if the linked buff is missing and a living, supported demon is within 100 yards. Both abilities honor **Defensives**. [Shadow Ward](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/6229) and [Soul Link](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/19028).

**Saving your demon.** From level 12, with **Defensives** enabled, Health Funnel is the first recommendation when your living demon reaches 30% health during an engaged outdoor PvE fight. The same rule applies solo and in a party. You can keep the mob selected and stand within 20 yards of your pet. Your own health can be low if WoW says you can start the spell. A demon in danger takes priority over Drain Life, Drain Soul and self-healing items. If you are already draining, the icon can prompt you to switch to Funnel. Each tick heals the demon, and you choose when to stop the channel. When pet health is protected, WoW controls when Classic shows the icon. [Health Funnel](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/755) and [Affliction leveling guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/affliction/dps-leveling-tips).

Between pulls, Hekili suggests Funnel at 55% pet health or below if you have at least 80% health and a full channel would leave at least 60%. WoW must supply a readable player maximum to calculate that reserve. The Affliction guide also recommends bandaging or resummoning between pulls. [Affliction leveling guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/affliction/dps-leveling-tips).

**Preparation and shards.** Between pulls, the packs can suggest creating a missing level-appropriate Healthstone, renewing Demon Skin or Demon Armor, or summoning a missing demon. With Demonic Pact a different summon preserves the sacrifice buff. In battlegrounds and arenas, a learned Felhunter takes priority when a shard is available. Healthstone creation costs one confirmed shard. Life Tap and Demonic Rune account for their projected health costs when health is readable. Stationary sustained boss damage follows the upstream mana priority while health is hidden. [Create Healthstone ranks](https://mattjcr.github.io/wowforeverultimatecalculator/en/classes/warlock/abilities/create-healthstone/).

**Finishing with Drain Soul.** From level 10, a trained Drain Soul can finish an engaged, non-trivial field or dungeon enemy when the Warlock carries fewer than two shards. At 25% target health or below, Classic gives Drain Soul priority even when Corruption or Bane of Agony is missing. The guides recommend finishing a dying enemy for a shard and leave the percentage to the player. WoW controls the icon’s visibility when target health is hidden. If the global cooldown is the only thing delaying Drain Soul, Classic keeps its icon and key visible with a cooldown swipe. You can also start the drain yourself. If the same enemy survives a full channel and you still have the same shard count, Hekili prioritizes continuing Drain Soul over refreshing DoTs. A shard arriving in the bags, canceling the channel, starting another spell, moving or changing targets ends that priority. The finisher requires standing still, spell range and enough mana. A kill during the channel awards a shard on an eligible target. A tick can also award one. [Wowhead’s leveling tips](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/affliction/dps-leveling-tips) and [Icy Veins’ Soul Shard guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/affliction-warlock-leveling).

**Racials.** Orc Blood Fury, Troll Berserking and Gnome Eureka follow **Cooldowns**. The packs hold these damage cooldowns during movement or observed incapacitation so their short buffs are available when casting resumes. The control-breaking racials require a matching observed effect. Shatter Curse needs a readable curse. Rapid Regeneration can help a wounded Troll between pulls. Cannibalize requires a stationary player in need of recovery and a selected hostile Humanoid or Undead corpse in reach, including a fallen battleground opponent when its eligibility is visible. Moving, taking another action or taking damage ends Cannibalize. The addon follows the client's channel stop, and any next spell it shows during the channel is timed for its end. [Cannibalize activation](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/20577) and [channel effect](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/20578). [Forever's racial changes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-november-2/2360696/7) and [WoWSims racial implementation](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/ab0f00e4fc83/sim/core/racials.go).

**Player combat.** In the open world, battlegrounds and arenas, damage requires a living, attackable player already in combat and confirmed range for the spell. Curse of Tongues can answer an observed cast. Learned Curse of Exhaustion can slow a moving opponent in confirmed melee range. The packs use learned instant spells and procs while moving, use Shadowburn as a finisher with a shard, and hold damage while the selected opponent's Fear or Howl of Terror is observed. When Forever hides control, the player judges when to resume damage. Timing Fear, Banish, pet commands, Spell Lock and Devour Magic remains the player's decision. [SM Ruin PvP guide](https://thetirisfalen.com/wow-forever/guides/warlock-sm-ruin-pvp/).

## Keeping current with upstream

`origin` is the fork. `upstream` is [zmsl/hekili](https://github.com/zmsl/hekili). Work on the fork's `forever` branch. When you want upstream updates, pull them into that branch from a clean checkout.

```sh
git switch forever
git pull --no-rebase upstream forever
```

If a pull touches a rotation we've extended, keep upstream's updated max-level damage priority and carry forward the leveling and PvE rules. Regenerate affected packs from their sources, then run the checks in the development guide. The game links point into the checkout, so finish the merge and checks before reloading in WoW.

## Testing in WoW Forever

Link each `src/<Addon>` directory containing `<Addon>.toc` as a separate entry under the client's `Interface/AddOns`. Link `Hekili`, `Hekili_Shared`, every class pack, `LibHiddenValues` and `LibTimeToDie`. On Linux use directory symlinks. For Windows and WSL see the [upstream setup instructions](../../DEVELOPMENT.md#link-the-addons-into-the-game-client).

When using Bottles via Flatpak, grant Bottles read-only access to the checkout's `src/` so it can follow the links. Restart Bottles after granting access and fully restart WoW when adding addon folders. For subsequent edits to existing Lua files, type `/reload` and use `/hekili` to check the addon. If editing an APL or recipe, regenerate its runtime pack before reloading. Keep the linked Hekili addons out of the addon manager's update cycle so it does not replace the links.
